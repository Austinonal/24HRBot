> Create one recurring research routine every day at 08:00 in `Asia/Bangkok`. Use the instructions in my persistent `research/SKILL.md` and the scope in `research/PROJECT_BRIEF.md`. Check for material changes since the last successful run, use current primary sources and independent corroboration, append source records and findings to the persistent research archive, compact the rolling memory, write a dated Thai report, and send me a short summary with links. If no meaningful changes are found, tell me that without repeating old material. Do not create new tasks automatically. If the AI provider, search tool or data source is unavailable, record the blocker and stop instead of retrying indefinitely.

Change the time/frequency and topic to match your needs. Confirm the Routine timezone and next run shown by OpenMausBot after saving.
